# PurePlate

A food delivery app concept designed to provide a more transparent and convenient ordering experience.

## Overview

PurePlate addresses common food delivery concerns such as unclear delivery costs, limited local food choices, and uncertain delivery times.

The concept aims to help users discover, compare, and order food from a wider range of restaurants, including local culinary businesses.

## Key Highlights

- Restaurant and food discovery
- Category-based browsing and filtering
- Transparent ordering experience
- Real-time order tracking
- Favorite restaurants and re-ordering
